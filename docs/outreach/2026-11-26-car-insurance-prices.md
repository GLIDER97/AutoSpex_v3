# Outreach drafts: car insurance prices from official BLS data (26 Nov 2026)

**Piece:** https://www.myautospex.com/insurance-estimator. The car insurance calculator now has a guide built on the Bureau of Labor Statistics consumer price index for motor vehicle insurance, January 2017 to October 2026 (BLS data to October 2026, published November 2026). It has 3 embeddable charts, a CSV of all 118 months, and a "How to cite this" line.

**How these are used (decision 8):**
- The owner's main session saves each email below as a **Gmail draft**. The owner reviews, edits and sends.
- The agent never sends anything.
- All targets are US outlets or US consumer groups (tier 1).
- Each email ends with the owner's backlink line (decision 8, 25 Nov), word for word, just above the signature.
- Replace `[Your name]` before sending.
- **Address check:** the daily run's sandbox can't open these sites, so every address comes from the outlet's own contact page as shown in web search results (linked).
  - **"From the outlet's page":** the page itself lists it.
  - **"Confirm first":** two of the outlet's pages disagree, or the address wasn't shown. Check the outlet's site before sending.

**Findings used (all on the page, computed from BLS CPI-U, US city average, not seasonally adjusted):**
- Car insurance prices rose 51.4% from October 2021 to October 2026; overall consumer prices rose 22.0%.
- Prices peaked in February 2026 and were 4.8% lower by October 2026. Over the 12 months to September 2026 they fell 5.0% (October 2025 wasn't published because of the federal funding lapse).
- From December 2021 to December 2024 prices rose 52.9%, including 20.3% in 2023 alone.
- Over the same five years: car maintenance and repair +45.0%, gasoline +31.9%, new vehicles +10.8%, used cars and trucks −7.4%.
- Since January 2017: car insurance +67.3%, all items +39.0%.
- Caveat in the emails where it matters: this is a national price index, not dollar premiums, and it isn't broken down by state.

**Added 28 Nov, before any were sent:** each email now has a P.S. about the new silo page, `/car-insurance/average-cost` (BLS household spending on car insurance, by region, age and income). That page's outreach goes out with this batch, so no outlet is pitched twice in one week.

**Embeddable charts:** `/embed/chart/car-insurance-price-trend`, `/embed/chart/car-insurance-yearly-change`, `/embed/chart/driving-costs-5-years`. Each page's "Embed this chart" box gives the snippet with the credit link.
