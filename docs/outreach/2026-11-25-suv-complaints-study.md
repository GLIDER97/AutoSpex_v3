**Findings used (all from the study page, sourced to NHTSA):**
- The 2017 Ford Escape has the most complaints of 160 model-years: 2,714. Next are the 2016 Ford Explorer (2,467) and the 2018 Honda CR-V (2,172).
- Ford Escape is the most complained-about model across 2015–2022 (9,665), then the Ford Explorer (9,053) and the Jeep Cherokee (7,526).
- 26% of the 83,871 complaints name the engine.
- 960 complaints report a fire, the most for the 2016 Kia Sorento (50).
- Owners filed 645 complaints in 2025 about the 2017 Escape alone.
- Caveat in every email: the counts are raw, not adjusted for sales.
